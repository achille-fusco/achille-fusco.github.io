## Research interests

- Natural Language Processing and language models
- Computational and cognitive modeling of language
- Linguistically informed neural architectures
- Language model evaluation
- Tokenization and morphological representation
- Formal semantics and the syntax–semantics interface
- Computational dialectology and linguistic resources

## Selected recent work

My recent work includes research on morphology-aware and linguistically motivated tokenization in BabyLM, recurrent neural networks trained on child-directed speech, evaluation resources for Italian language models, and a semi-automatic annotation pipeline for the Manzini–Savoia corpus of Italo-Romance dialects.

I also work on formal semantic analyses of attitude predicates and infinitival complementation, with a particular focus on the interaction between syntactic structure, modality, belief, and intention.
